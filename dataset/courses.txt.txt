Academic Courses

Software Design and Architecture:
Topics studied include object-oriented design, SOLID principles,
design patterns, MVC architecture, client-server architecture,
and microservices.

Artificial Intelligence:
Topics include machine learning concepts, classification,
clustering, Naive Bayes, decision trees and related AI techniques.

Natural Language Processing:
Topics include tokenization, stemming, lemmatization,
bag-of-words, TF-IDF, n-grams, language models, LSTM,
Naive Bayes and text classification, Euclidean Similarity,
Overlap Similarity and Dice Similarity.

Information Retrieval:
Topics include inverted indexes, TF-IDF, cosine similarity,
Jaccard similarity, PageRank, HITS and index compression.

Data Mining:
Topics include data preprocessing, data mining techniques,
clustering, K-Means, K-Medoids, hierarchical clustering,
DBSCAN, classification, Naive Bayes, decision trees,
confusion matrices and distance calculations.
